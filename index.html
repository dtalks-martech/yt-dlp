Experiment | make the optional debrief more visible
Hypothesis: a small form-level choice, reinforced in Email 2, increases qualified conversations without materially hurting downloads.
HYPOTHESIS
Adding a clear “Request a 15-min maturity debrief” checkbox on the form and mentioning it in Email 2 will increase the rate of qualified conversations without materially reducing overall download volume.
TEST WINDOW
~2–3 weeks
Or until a meaningful sample of form completes is available.
PROVISIONAL SUCCESS CRITERIA
The percentage of downloads that request a debrief (or are marked qualified by sales) is higher than the baseline period, while total downloads do not drop more than a small acceptable threshold—set after first-week volume is visible.
No fixed absolute target is assumed before the baseline is known.

4 · LAUNCH & MEASURE
Experiment decision rules and forecasting discipline
Make the next move from relative performance and sales feedback—not invented forecasts.
CONTINUE
Debrief request rate rises and sales confirms the leads are usable.
ADJUST
Volume holds but quality is low → tighten role / company questions or the debrief talk track.
STOP / REVERT
Download volume falls sharply or sales feedback is negative on lead quality → remove / rephrase the checkbox and test a softer CTA.
ASSUMPTIONS / NO INVENTED FORECASTS
Do not project absolute traffic or pipeline numbers. Judge success on relative movement in the scorecard metrics and qualitative sales feedback, given organic-only distribution and an unknown current baseline.
